# English Summary

## Enterprise AI Golfer

**Experience-Driven Decision & Capability Framework**

Enterprise AI Golfer is a business-centered methodology for moving from AI assistance to accountable agent execution. It uses golf course management as a shared language:

- **AI Coach** defines goals, training, governance and brand guardrails.
- **AI Golfer** observes the task, makes an authorized decision, selects models and tools, executes a workflow, and accepts verification.
- **VAD** maps the task environment.
- **Memory** preserves traceable facts and experience.
- **Workflow** turns a decision into visible action.
- **Verify** measures quality, risk, cost and business outcomes.
- **VAC** retains verified methods as reusable enterprise capabilities.

The GOLFER loop stands for **Goal & Brand, Observe, Learn, Form the Decision, Execute, Review**, followed by retaining successful methods as VAC.

> Models are clubs. Tools are equipment. The durable asset is the enterprise's decision capability.
